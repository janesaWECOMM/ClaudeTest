Prompt Writing Tips

Be Specific

Instead of "a dog", try "a golden retriever puppy with fluffy fur playing in autumn leaves"

Describe the Scene

Include environment details like "in a cozy coffee shop" or "on a misty mountain peak at dawn"

Specify the Medium

Define the art style: "oil painting", "digital illustration", "35mm photograph", "anime style"

Add Technical Details

Include camera settings, lighting conditions, or render engine for more control

Use Weights

Some generators support weighting like (important:1.5) to emphasize certain elements

Negative Prompts Help

Tell the AI what to avoid: "no blur, no watermarks, no extra limbs, no distortion"
